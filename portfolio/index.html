Focus

Research agenda

I build AI systems for underrepresented languages, where safety and reliability failures are the least visible and the least studied. Most AI safety and evaluation infrastructure is built and tested in high-resource languages. That leaves models in Uzbek, Karakalpak and related Turkic languages effectively unaudited when they are deployed in high-stakes settings. My work closes that gap from both ends: I build the language resources that evaluation depends on, and I ship systems where low-resource failure is treated as a first-class risk from day one.

CustomerSignal AI

Multilingual banking intelligence, fully local and API-free

  • 0.903 macro-F1
  • 0.884 critical-intent recall
  • 31/31 regression tests
  • en · ru · uz · kaa
  • Classifies customer requests across 15 banking intents in English, Russian, Uzbek and Karakalpak. Critical-intent recall is tracked separately because a missed intent there is a compliance or safety failure.
  • Leakage-safe evaluation that splits on source_id, so reported scores reflect real generalisation instead of memorised overlap.
  • Confidence calibration with manual-review escalation: low-confidence or high-stakes outputs go to a human instead of being auto-resolved.
  • PII masking (regex plus Luhn checksum) and citation-grounded RAG over a 127-chunk policy base with hybrid dense–sparse retrieval, so every answer can be checked against source policy.
  • Integrates a fine-tuned Karakalpak Whisper ASR beta model, one of very few speech recognition systems for the language.
